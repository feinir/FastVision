# FastVision Privacy Policy

FastVision is a macOS browser and editor for local images and videos.

## Files and settings

FastVision reads files and folders you select to display images, generate previews, show file metadata, and find similar photos. It writes edited copies or changes to files when you choose to save, replace, move, or delete them. The Mac App Store version stores folder permissions as security-scoped bookmarks. Settings and browsing data used by the app are stored on your Mac.

## Data collection

FastVision does not require an account. The developer does not collect your photos, videos, browsing activity, or identity information through the app. FastVision does not contain advertising or analytics trackers.

If you choose Share, macOS passes the files you selected to the service you choose. Opening a link or map hands off to the relevant app or website. Opening a map passes the selected location to the map service. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

FastVision does not retain data on a server. You can remove local preferences, cached data, and saved folder permissions by removing the app’s local data from your Mac.

## Questions

You can contact the project through the [FastVision support page](https://github.com/feinir/FastVision/issues). Please avoid including personal photos or paths in public reports.
